International goods returns - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for international goods returns. Customs evidence and commercial courier surcharges answer different parts of a shipment request.
The carrier charges appendix sets a EUR 23 customs-document correction surcharge; the customs instruction contains no carrier prices.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
