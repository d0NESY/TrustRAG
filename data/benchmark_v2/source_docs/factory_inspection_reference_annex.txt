Incoming component inspection - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for incoming component inspection. The quality instruction determines release status, while commercial claims determine recoverable costs.
The commercial claims appendix permits recovery of actual documented freight after rejection; the inspection instruction assigns no supplier charge.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
