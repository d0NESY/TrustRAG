Rail travel reservations - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for rail travel reservations. Fare rights govern the carrier ticket. Reimbursement deadlines are set by travel accounts.
The travel accounts guide gives thirty-two calendar days to submit a rail receipt; fare conditions do not set expense deadlines.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
