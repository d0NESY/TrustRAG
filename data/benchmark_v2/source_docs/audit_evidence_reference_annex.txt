Audit evidence transfer - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for audit evidence transfer. Transfer controls define access and handling. Record lifetimes are set in the assurance records schedule.
The assurance records schedule retains signed management responses for eight years; the transfer procedure sets no archive period.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
