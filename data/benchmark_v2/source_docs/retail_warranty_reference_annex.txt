Retail accessory warranties - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for retail accessory warranties. A fault category is not a warranty duration. Delivery reimbursements remain governed by the delivery appendix.
The retail delivery appendix refunds actual standard postage for a confirmed defect; the fault assessment guide does not set delivery compensation.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
