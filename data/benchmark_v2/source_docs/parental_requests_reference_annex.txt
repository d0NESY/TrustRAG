Family leave case management - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for family leave case management. Entitlement cases are handled under the family leave policy; evidence storage follows the People records annex.
The People records annex retains adoption eligibility evidence for eighteen months; the leave case policy states eligibility but not storage duration.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
