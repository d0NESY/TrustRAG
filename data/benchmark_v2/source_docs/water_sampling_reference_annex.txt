Process water sampling - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for process water sampling. Collection frequency is a sampling-plan rule. Waste routing and release specifications are distinct references.
The laboratory waste annex assigns failed release samples to disposal route BIO-12; the sampling plan does not classify waste.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
