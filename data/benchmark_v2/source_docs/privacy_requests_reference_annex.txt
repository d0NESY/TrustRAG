Data subject request intake - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for data subject request intake. Identity verification and response processing are intake duties; retention is controlled by a separate evidence schedule.
The privacy evidence schedule retains identity-check evidence for fourteen months after closure; the intake standard sets process timing only.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
