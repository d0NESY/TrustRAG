Accessible event planning - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for accessible event planning. Participation support is defined by the event policy; purchased services are priced by the supplier rate card.
The access supplier rate card lists a GBP 40 interpreter cancellation fee; the events policy does not set supplier rates.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
