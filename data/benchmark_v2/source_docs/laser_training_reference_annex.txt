Laser laboratory qualification - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for laser laboratory qualification. Training records and equipment inspection records have different owners and must not be substituted for one another.
The optical equipment annex requires protective eyewear inspection every six weeks; the training rule governs people rather than equipment.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
