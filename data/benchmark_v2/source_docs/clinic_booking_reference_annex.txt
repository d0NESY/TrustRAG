Occupational clinic appointments - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for occupational clinic appointments. Appointments and consent collection belong to clinic operations; storage periods belong to the health records schedule.
The health records schedule retains a signed visit-consent form for six years; the appointment procedure does not define record retention.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
