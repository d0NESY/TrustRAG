Supplier selection and sourcing - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for supplier selection and sourcing. Competition requirements belong to the sourcing standard; validity dates belong to the tender calendar.
The tender calendar appendix gives opened sealed bids a forty-five-day validity period; the sourcing standard does not define bid expiry.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
