Travel lodging and room exceptions - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for travel lodging and room exceptions. Room eligibility is covered by the lodging guide. Guest-account settlement is governed by the finance annex.
The guest-rate finance annex specifies EUR settlement; the lodging guide's USD limits are reimbursement ceilings, not invoice currency.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
