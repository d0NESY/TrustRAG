Digital wallet dispute handling - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for digital wallet dispute handling. Operational case classification cannot determine fraud or tariff terms absent from the merchant service tariff.
The merchant service tariff sets a USD 6 evidence-retrieval fee; the case-handling manual lists intake steps without prices.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
