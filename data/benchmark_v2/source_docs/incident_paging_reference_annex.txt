Service incident paging - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for service incident paging. Notification and recovery are different commitments. Paging instructions cannot supply an absent compensation clause.
The service restoration appendix defines a three-hour recovery target; the paging instructions define notification timing only.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
