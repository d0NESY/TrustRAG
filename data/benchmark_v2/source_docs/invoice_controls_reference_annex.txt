Supplier invoice controls - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for supplier invoice controls. Invoice matching, payment terms and currency conversion are separate decisions; conversion is a treasury responsibility.
The treasury conversion memo uses the settlement-day rate for foreign service invoices; the invoice control guide sets matching checks only.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
