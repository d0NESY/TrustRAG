Managed router replacement - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for managed router replacement. Activation approval is technical. Transit insurance belongs to logistics and cannot be inferred from network diagnostics.
The hardware transit appendix provides a EUR 900 courier insurance limit per replacement router; the activation procedure defines no insurance value.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
