Field equipment repair visits - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for field equipment repair visits. Technical kits, customer access and tariff terms must be traced to their separate controlling references.
The field service tariff sets a USD 75 charge for a cancelled escorted visit; the repair instruction states no customer cancellation price.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
