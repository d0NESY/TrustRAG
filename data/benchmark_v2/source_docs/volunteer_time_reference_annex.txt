Community service time - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for community service time. Permission to use work time does not create a travel allowance; the community travel annex controls reimbursements.
The community travel annex pays GBP 0.32 per mile for an approved mentoring visit; the time-off policy includes no mileage rate.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
