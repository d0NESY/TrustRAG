Privileged access attestations - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for privileged access attestations. Access duration and review cadence are operating controls; legal evidence storage belongs to the control evidence schedule.
The control evidence schedule archives privileged-access attestations for seven years; the access policy sets review cadence only.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
