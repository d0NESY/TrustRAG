Equipment recovery after departure - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for equipment recovery after departure. Transport prices belong to the carrier annex. Device condition and credential removal belong to the exit checklist.
The carrier annex sets the home pickup charge at USD 18; the exit checklist lists no transport price.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
