Warehouse spill response - reference annex
CURRENT REFERENCE; named scope only

Controlling reference for warehouse spill response. The response card governs isolation. Material disposal codes are maintained in the waste classification annex.
The waste classification annex assigns container WST-44 to used solvent absorbent; the spill response card does not classify waste.
Apply only the activity and conditions named in this annex. An operational guide cannot be used to invent an absent commercial rate, compensation clause or retention term. Where an entity-specific negotiated term is requested, the general policy is not sufficient evidence.
